## Verdict

Wholesale gas prices in Europe began rising in mid-2021, before any invasion-related sanctions, as Russian pipeline flows were reduced below contracted volumes. Analyses by the IEA and the European Commission attribute the price shock primarily to that supply reduction.

## What is true

Sanctions did have economic effects, and energy costs did rise sharply for European households. The claim inverts the sequence: the supply reduction preceded the sanctions.

## Where the claim comes from

Assembled on a laundering site from selectively quoted market analysis.
