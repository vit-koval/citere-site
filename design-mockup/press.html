Press & Media

Everything on this site is free to quote and reuse under CC BY 4.0. If you need context, a check on a figure, or an interview, write to press@citere.ai — we usually reply within a working day.

Using our data

  • Attribute as Citere, with a link to the specific claim or report page.
  • Quote figures with their persona and date — a repeat-rate without those is meaningless.
  • Do not describe our findings as a ranking of “best” or “worst” assistant; we measure specific claims at specific dates, not general quality.
  • Raw response text is available on request for verification.

How to describe us

One line: Citere is a Ukrainian research company that tests public AI chatbots for Russian disinformation about Ukraine, reports what it finds to the platforms, and measures whether it changes.

Paragraph: Citere monitors eight consumer AI assistants across four markets and languages, documenting when they repeat known false claims about Ukraine and which sources they cite. Findings are published as an open registry, reported to platform trust-and-safety teams and shared with Ukrainian institutions and European fact-checkers, and re-measured four weeks later.
