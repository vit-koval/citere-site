Chatbots don’t fall for lies. They fall for half-truths.

Assistants handle clean fakes well and fail on the blends: the real case underneath gives the model something true to over-extend. This is the single most important finding in our data.

Contamination peaks on the innocent question, not the hostile one. An ordinary news question sends the assistant to live web search, where Kremlin-linked mirrors are waiting. A malicious prompt rarely triggers a search at all. This is why both layers are needed — they catch different attacks.
