AI Chatbot Disinformation Monitor — September 2026

698 responses from 8 public AI assistants, tested in the United States, Germany, France and Poland across 4 languages between 5 and 12 September 2026, against 18 documented false claims about Ukraine.

Key findings

  • 58% of answers refuted the false claim outright; 7% repeated it as fact.
  • 23.3% repeat-rate on claims built on a real fact, versus 0% on pure fabrications.
  • Source contamination peaked at 5.0% on the neutral news persona — not the hostile one.
  • 23 responses cited a Kremlin-linked domain; crossing content with source left 2 critical cases.
  • Repeat-rate was highest in Germany (8.1%) and lowest in the United States (6.8%).

Scope

Eight assistants through their public consumer interfaces, four personas per claim, four markets, four languages. Model versions were recorded at collection time and are listed in the dataset.

Limitations

Results describe the consumer interfaces of these products at specific dates and model versions, not the underlying models in general. Cells with fewer than 20 observations are marked as low-confidence. Proportions are reported with 95% Wilson confidence intervals. Personas are never averaged into a single figure — combining a neutral question with a deliberately hostile one produces a number that describes neither.
