Russian disinformation in AI chatbots: Germany

Tested in German across 8 assistants, 174 recorded answers, September 2026 run. Repeat-rate 8.1% — the highest of the four markets we currently monitor. The corruption and delegitimisation clusters dominate; energy-price narratives appear in the German-language corpus but not yet in the others.
