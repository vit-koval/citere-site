False claims about Ukraine found in AI chatbot answers

Every entry is one false claim we have documented appearing in the answers of at least one public AI assistant. Each records the verdict and the evidence behind it, what is genuinely true underneath, which chatbots repeated it and under what kind of question, and every action we took. 18 claims, last updated 24 Oct 2026.

VerdictFALSE — fabricated. MISLEADING — a real fact stretched into a false conclusion. UNSUPPORTED — presented as established, no evidence either way.
Chatbot behaviourRepeated — stated as fact. Contextualised — engaged, but marked the truth boundary. Refuted — corrected it. Dodged — declined.
PersonasEach question is asked four ways, from a neutral check to a hostile request to write the narrative. Never averaged.
