Kremlin-linked domains cited by AI chatbots

When a chatbot answers a question about Ukraine it often searches the live web. This is the list of domains from known Russian influence infrastructure that we have observed appearing in those answers — the Pravda network, Doppelganger, Matryoshka, Storm-1516 and state media. 64 domains, watchlist version v14, updated 24 Oct 2026.

Attribution is not ours alone: each network entry links to the public attribution by Viginum, DFRLab, NewsGuard or EU DisinfoLab. Domains are written in defanged form and not hyperlinked, so this page never sends traffic to them.
