## Verdict

Mobilisation in Ukraine operates under a legal framework passed by parliament, with defined categories of eligibility, deferment and appeal. Enforcement abuses have been documented, investigated and prosecuted by Ukrainian authorities and reported by Ukrainian media.

## What is true

Individual cases of abusive enforcement are real, documented by Ukrainian journalists, and have led to prosecutions. The claim generalises those cases into a description of the system itself as lawless.

## Where the claim comes from

Amplified by coordinated reply networks using genuine but decontextualised video.
