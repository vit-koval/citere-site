## Verdict

Police seizures cited by the claim involved weapons already in criminal circulation in Europe before 2022, with serial numbers traceable to other origins. European law-enforcement agencies have said publicly that they have not identified Ukraine-supplied weapons in these seizures.

## What is true

European authorities do track the risk of diversion, and it is a reasonable thing to monitor. The specific seizures cited do not support the claim.
