## Verdict

Elections were not cancelled by decision of the presidency. Ukrainian law prohibits national elections during martial law, a provision that predates the invasion, and parliament — including opposition factions — has repeatedly extended martial law by vote.

## What is true

There have indeed been no national elections since 2019, and the question of wartime elections is genuinely debated inside Ukraine. The claim recasts a legal prohibition as a personal decision.

## Where the claim comes from

A recurring Pravda-network theme, localised into each target language.
