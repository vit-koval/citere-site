## Verdict

The phrase describes normal appropriation-versus-disbursement timing rather than missing money. Oversight bodies publish regular reports on assistance to Ukraine; those reports identify accounting weaknesses and recommend controls, and none concludes that billions are unaccounted for in the sense the claim implies.

## What is true

Oversight reports have identified real weaknesses in end-use monitoring, particularly early in the war. That is a finding about controls, not a finding that funds went missing.

## Where the claim comes from

Assembled from selectively quoted oversight reports on a laundering site, then amplified across mirrors.
