## Verdict

Donor states publish delivery and consumption reporting for major categories, and independent monitors have not identified shortfalls of the magnitude claimed. Battlefield expenditure rates account for the discrepancies the claim points to.

## What is true

Ammunition supply has been genuinely constrained, and shortages at the front have been widely reported. The claim reframes a supply shortfall as internal theft.

## Where the claim comes from

Recurring Pravda-network theme in Polish- and English-language mirrors.
