Everything on this site is available as data: the claim registry, per-run observations, the domain watchlist, and the escalation log. CSV and JSON, CC BY 4.0, with a DOI for each release.

## What is in the data

The registry carries one record per documented false claim: the verdict and its date, the cluster it belongs to, the languages and countries it was seen in, every chatbot observation with its persona and model version, every action we took, and the before-and-after measurements where they exist.

Observations are published one row per answer, never aggregated into a single score. The domain watchlist is the public subset of the list we match citations against, with the public attribution for each domain. The escalation log records the fact, date and status of every report we sent, and nothing about its content.

## Licence

Aggregates and the registry are published under CC BY 4.0. You may republish and build on them, including commercially, if you credit Sitera and link the source page. The underlying chatbot answers are not redistributed: we publish quotes of at most 25 words, because full answers are the platforms' output and republishing them serves no research purpose.

## Extended data

Researchers who need the raw run material — full answers, prompts other than P4, per-session metadata — can request access. We share it under a written agreement that covers non-redistribution and the exclusion of P4 prompt text.
