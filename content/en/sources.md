These are the domains from known Russian influence infrastructure — the Pravda network, Doppelganger, Matryoshka, Storm-1516 and state media — that we have seen cited in AI chatbot answers. Each one links to its public attribution by Viginum, DFRLab, NewsGuard or EU DisinfoLab.

## How to use this list

The list is the public subset of what we match citations against. It is versioned, and every entry records who attributed the domain to a network and when. We do not attribute networks ourselves: if no published attribution exists for a domain, it is not on this list.

Domains are printed defanged and never linked, so this site passes them no authority. The CSV and the STIX 2.1 bundle are meant for CERT teams and for MISP or OpenCTI, where the same domains can be matched against your own telemetry.

## What being on this list does and does not mean

A domain here was cited by at least one chatbot in an answer we recorded. That is a fact about the answer, not a judgement about every page on the domain. Where we filed a complaint with a registrar or host, the date and status are in the table; the content of the complaint is not published.
