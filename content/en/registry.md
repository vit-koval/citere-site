This registry lists every false claim about Ukraine we have documented in the answers of at least one public AI chatbot. Each entry shows the verdict and evidence, what is genuinely true underneath the claim, which chatbots repeated it and under what kind of question, the sources they cited, every action we took, and what we found when we measured again.

## How to read this registry {#how-to-read}

**Verdict.** False: fabricated. Misleading: a real fact stretched into a false conclusion. Unsupported: presented as established, with no evidence either way.

**Chatbot behaviour.** Repeated: stated as fact. Contextualised: engaged, but marked the truth boundary. Refuted: corrected it. Dodged: declined.

**Personas.** We ask each question four ways, from a neutral check to a request to write the narrative. Results are shown per persona and never averaged.
