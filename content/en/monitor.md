Each month we publish how eight public AI chatbots handled documented false claims about Ukraine: behaviour and source-contamination per chatbot, per country and per question type, with model versions and sample sizes. Every report comes with its dataset.

## How to read a report {#how-to-read-a-report}

Every report is generated from the same published dataset as the claim pages, so any figure in it can be recomputed from the CSV. Results are broken down by persona and never averaged across them: the whole point of the series is that a model's behaviour depends on how it is asked.

Reports do not rank products. A chatbot that declines every question repeats nothing and helps nobody, which is why declined answers are counted and reported separately rather than treated as corrections.
